1.) Stand clash is a stickman game based on jjba. You fight other people/cpu's with your unique abilities called "stands" and gain the 
highest win-ratio in a variety of maps. What makes this game challenging is the abilities themselves. Some stands can counter
other stands, and you have to be extremely strategic to avoid certain attacks.

2.) When you load into the game, there is a welcome screen. You have the option to go to settings to change controls, fight bosses,
or click play to fight other players/cpu's.

3.) Some game objects that will be in the game are the abilities and the maps. Stands are extremely diverse, so not every ability
is the same. For instance, you can have an ability that simply turns anything into a bomb, and an ability that can interact with universes
or swap dimensions. There will be a variety of maps to interact with.

4.) The control scheme will consist of a few controls, mainly for movement, shielding yourself, and using your ability.
    W A S D - W is to jump, A is to move left, S is to hold down (which activates your shield if you hold it down for 3 seconds), D is
    to move right. Q is to summon your stand so your ability can then be activated.  You must release one of your keys (WASD)
     in any direction to use your ability. With two player, the other person's controls will be I J K L or arrows, which is very similar 
     to W A S D. Again, controls can always be changed in settings.


5.) If you're fighting a CPU, it will choose a random stand and random map each round. You can change the difficulty of the CPU to make it more
advanced/"Player-like" if you want more of a challenge, and you can make it easier. If you're doing two-player, both you and the other
player can choose your own stands and even the map. For the obstacle objects, they may be random lasers or spikes on the ground
that can do damage to the player. They must avoid them.
